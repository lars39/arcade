SkyriftBrawl

20 fighters · 20 floating arenas · knock them off the edge

Every fighter has 3 signature moves and a super that can be used only twice per match. No weapons to pick up. Keyboard, gamepad and touch all work.

How to play

Hits raise a rival's damage. The higher it climbs, the farther they fly. Knock them past the edge of the screen to take a stock. Last fighter standing wins.

PS5 and Xbox controllers

In menus, the d-pad or stick moves, Cross / A selects and Circle / B goes back. The first controller plays as Player 1, the second as Player 2. Hits make the controller rumble. On a phone, turn it sideways and use the on-screen buttons.

Moves marked as recovery (like rising uppercuts) work once per trip through the air, so save one to get back to the stage.
